I wanted a pair of shorts that had a fall front with a double row of buttons, a front pleat, and a flared
bottom edge gathered down into a cuff. So I made one!

The flare is basically what you get when you take a Titan, shorten it, and hack it partway into a circle
skirt. This pattern can be short or long, flared or unflared, and cuffed or uncuffed, so you can produce
quite a wide variety of different pants. Only the fall front construction is non-optional.

I have no idea how this pattern will behave if you have a belly. If that describes you and
you decide to make it, tell me how it goes!
