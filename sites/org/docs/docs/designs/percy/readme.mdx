## What You Need {#needs}

To make percy, you will need the following:

- [Basic sewing supplies](/docs/sewing/basic-sewing-supplies)
- About 1 - 3 metres (1.1 - 3.3 yards) of a suitable main fabric (see [Fabric
  options](#fabric))
- Some interfacing for the waistband and cuffs
- 4 or 6 buttons for the waistband
- (optional) 2 or 4 buttons for the cufs (purely ornamental)

Due to the many different styles of percy it is difficult to precisely say what
you need so we will go through some of the options.

## Cutting Instructions {#cutting}

**Main Fabric**

- Cut **2 backs**, mirrored
- Cut **2 fronts**, mirrored
- Cut **2 waistband backs**
- Cut **2 waistband fronts**
- Cut **4 waistband sides**
- Cut **2 cuffs**
- Cut **2 pockets**
- Cut **2 pocket facings**

**Interfacing**

- Cut **1 waistband back**
- Cut **2 waistband sides**
- Cut **1 waistband front**
