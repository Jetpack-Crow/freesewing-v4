- cut out all the pieces
- zigzag stitch the bottom edges of the pockets and pocket facings
- baste the front pleat
- sew pocket facings to front along the pocket curve line, right sides together
- trim and understitch seam allowance to pocket facing
- baste top of pocket facing to top of front
- attach pocket bags to pocket facings, leaving the inner edge open above the opening notch
- baste pocket bags and facings to outseam
- sew outseams right sides together
- sew inseams right sides together (resulting in 2 leg tubes)
- put 1 tube inside out around the other tube and sew the cross seam
- interface 1 of the waist front pieces, 2 of the waist side pieces, 1 of the waist back pieces (on the wrong side)
- put interfaced + non-interfaced front pieces together (right sides facing) and sew the top and sides
- sew the remaining interfaced waist pieces and remaining uninterfaced waist pieces together, side-back-side, into 2 long strips
- sew the waist strips together right sides facing at the top and sides
- attach and finish the waist front and waist back
- run a gathering thread along the whole bottom of the leg
- gather it to the length of the leg cuff
- attach and finish the leg cuff
- do that to the other leg
- sew the buttonholes on the front panel
- put it on and pin the front panel overlap how you want it
- mark through the buttonholes where the buttons should go
- take off the pants and sew the buttons on at those marks
